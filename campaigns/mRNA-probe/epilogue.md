# Epilogue

The last session log ends in the [Solomons](../../directory/Solomons.md).

## The dossier

- The Doomborgs compile the evidence recovered from the AI briefcase: the 512-bit training hash and the data routing that led to [Fomalhaut](../../directory/Fomalhaut/index.md).
- The trail points to [Transdyne Cybersystems](../../directory/TDC.md), and through them to [John McLeod](../../directory/TharsisMining/JohnMcLeod.md).
- The crew presents the dossier to [Matsuyama](../../directory/haruna/index.md).

## The ICO ruling

- The [ICO](../../directory/ico.md) finds TDC guilty of corporate espionage on [Tharsis Mining](../../directory/TharsisMining/index.md).
- TDC is fined.
- TDC is also sentenced to close its android factory in Fomalhaut, due to the links between that factory and John McLeod.
- A blockade is set around the factory.
- That blockade is the one [Gradient Descent](../gradient-descent/index.md) runs against.

## Perdurabo

- With McLeod exposed, [Tharsis Mining](../../directory/TharsisMining/index.md) takes over [Perdurabo](../../directory/Perdurabo/index.md) from [Haruna](../../directory/haruna/index.md).
- [Brightside Station](../../directory/Perdurabo/BrightsideStation.md) changes hands.
