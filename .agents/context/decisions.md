# Tier 2 — Campaign Rulings & Conversation Canon

Rulings, corporate protocols, and conversational decisions established with the GM. Outranked by Tier 1 published notes; outranks Tier 3 reference PDFs.

## Active Rulings

- **2026-10-23 — Terse Industrial Register:** In-setting campaign prose and gazetteer descriptions follow blue-collar sci-fi conventions:
  - Workplace realism, corporate dryness, slow dread, sensory grime, and ordinary workers surviving dangerous systems.
  - British English spelling with zaibatsu corporate bureaucracy.
  - Short, readable sentences (one sentence per line when practical).
- **2026-10-24 — Step 3 Session Prep Substitution (The Bends):** For Gradient Descent, Step 3 of session preparation (Regional Hazards & Misfortune Table) is replaced by the **BND (The Bends)** mechanic (*Gradient Descent*, p. 9). The GM rolls a Bends Check (d100 vs PC's Bends score) for each PC at the start of prep or appropriate transitions, logging any identity disintegration or psychological consequences directly into the briefing rather than generating an environmental hazard table.
- **2026-11-01 — Olmo's Android Composition (Minor Human, Major Machine):** Established at the table that [Olmo](../../directory/Fomalhaut/Mergulhadores/Olmo.md) is a hybrid android comprising a minor biological/human fraction and a major machine/robotic chassis. He operates on synthetic circuitry and mechanical actuators rather than systemic blood (vomits synthetic bile and dark oil under abdominal trauma). Triage requires mechanical and cybernetic repair procedures alongside specialised field medicine.
- **2026-11-01 — Floor 3.3 Hostility & Silus's Silence:** Following the violent destruction of Silus's unarmatured pseudoflesh construct ("a moça") in chamber 41A, [Silus](../../directory/Fomalhaut/Cloudbank/Silus.md) has ceased communications and the androids on Floor 3.3 are confirmed actively hostile to the dive team.
